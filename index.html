About Me

I am Ushindi Gabriel Matabaro, a bilingual Telecom Engineer, researcher and Emerging Technologies Educator from D.R. Congo. My work links advanced communication systems research with practical AI, robotics and ICT education for young people and institutions.

I hold an MSc in 5G and Emerging Communication Systems from the University of Essex and a BSc in Telecommunication Engineering. I focus on B5G resource allocation, network slicing, AI-RAN sustainability, digital literacy and responsible AI adoption across Sub-Saharan Africa.
